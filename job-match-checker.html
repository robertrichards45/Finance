How well does your résumé match the job?

Paste a job posting and your résumé. You'll get a match score, the key terms you're missing, and, for federal jobs, the USAJOBS checks reviewers care about.

🔒 Runs in your browser. Your résumé is never uploaded or saved.

A match score compares wording, not your worth as a candidate. Employers and applicant tracking systems each work differently, and no tool can guarantee an interview.

How to use your results

Mirror the real requirements

Where the posting's wording accurately describes work you've done, use it. Hiring software and reviewers both look for the requirements in the employer's own words.

Never pad with keywords

Adding skills you don't have can cost you the job, or worse, a federal referral. Every term should be backed by a real duty, tool or result.

Tailor for each application

The best résumé for one posting isn't the best for the next. CareerLaunch AI keeps a tailored version for each job you apply to.
