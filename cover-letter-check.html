What makes a cover letter work

One job, one letter

Name the role and the employer, and connect two or three of their requirements to things you've actually done.

Proof over adjectives

"Trained 14 new hires" says more than "great leader". Use real numbers you can explain in an interview.

Short and direct

Three or four paragraphs. End by asking for a conversation.
