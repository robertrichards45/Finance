What matters beyond the checklist?

Match the announcement

Use the actual duties and qualification requirements to decide which verified experience is most relevant. Do not paste keywords where they don't reflect your background.

Show evidence, not claims

Specific duties, scope, outcomes and tools you actually used are stronger than unsupported claims. If metrics are not known, describe the real work accurately.

Check the finished file

Open your final Word or PDF file to inspect dates, spacing, page count, contact information and any sections that moved when exported.
