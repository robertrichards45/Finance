Build your résumé from scratch — or make your existing one better

You don't need a finished résumé to get started. Choose a design, enter your actual work history, education, and skills, and tell us the kind of position you want. If you already have a résumé, you can bring it into the builder instead.

Three ways to get started

  1. Start from scratch. Add your name, target role, work experience, education, and relevant skills. Describe what you actually did in each position.
  2. Improve an existing résumé. Bring your résumé text into the builder and review the imported information for accuracy.
  3. Tailor to a specific job. Provide the job announcement, then connect your verified qualifications to its requirements.

What makes a strong résumé?

Focus on experience relevant to the role. Use clear job titles, employers, dates, specific duties, tools, and accomplishments you can verify. Describe results when you have reliable numbers, but never invent metrics, credentials, or employment history.

Federal or private-sector?

Private-sector résumés often emphasize concise achievements and skills. Federal applications must follow the vacancy's specific instructions, including specialized experience and required documents. Read our federal résumé guide if you are applying through USAJOBS.

Can I download before paying?

You can explore résumé designs and preview the builder, but completed AI-generated documents are only released after verified payment when checkout is available. The website is currently in prelaunch preview; no live checkout is open.
