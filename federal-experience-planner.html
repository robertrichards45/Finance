How to use it well

  1. Read the specialized-experience and duties sections of the specific job announcement.
  2. Break compound requirements into actual duties, skills and levels of responsibility.
  3. Include the position, dates, hours per week and concrete examples where the announcement calls for them.
  4. Use the outline to decide what belongs on your résumé. Keep your statements truthful.

For page formatting and other application requirements, read the federal résumé guide and free job-match checker. Always follow the vacancy announcement and official USAJOBS instructions.
