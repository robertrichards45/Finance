PRELAUNCH POLICIES

Room to get it right.

Document review

Review every factual claim, resolve verification questions, and confirm the six factual checks before approval. The portal supports draft corrections and up to three AI revision rounds per application.

Scope of revisions

Revisions should address the same target role and the facts you provided. A different target job or substantially different background is a new application. Nothing should add unsupported accomplishments, metrics, or qualifications.

Refunds

No payments are accepted during prelaunch, so there are no paid orders to refund. A final refund and cancellation policy, public support contact, and any applicable consumer rights must be established before checkout is activated. This page does not promise a paid refund program that is not yet operational.

Corrections and support

Use the portal’s support form for a failed generation, missing document, or correction request. Operational failures remain visible in the administrator dashboard. No guaranteed response or completion time is advertised.
